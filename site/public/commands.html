LoopifyBot commands

Every command the bot understands, with an example you can paste into Discord. Every / command also works with ! (!play, !skip…). Shortcuts like !p exist only with !.

Playback Queue Audio Extras

Playback

CommandWhat it doesExample
/play <song or link>Play from YouTube (search, video or playlist), SoundCloud or a link. Start with sc: or yt: to pick the site. Shortcut !p/play sc: city pop mix
/pausePause the current track./pause
/resumeResume playback./resume
/skipSkip to the next track.!skip
/previousGo back to the previous track. Shortcut !prev!prev
/stopStop playback and leave the voice channel. Shortcuts !dc !leave!leave
/nowplayingShow the track that is playing. Shortcuts !np !current!np

Queue

CommandWhat it doesExample
/queue [page]Show the queue, ten tracks per page. Shortcut !q/queue 2
/shuffleShuffle the queue./shuffle
/remove <position>Remove a track by its position in the queue./remove 3
/move <from> <to>Move a track to another position./move 5 1
/clearEmpty the queue. The current track keeps playing./clear
/loop [track|queue|off]Loop the track or the whole queue, or turn looping off./loop queue
/autoplayWhen the queue runs out, keep going with a related track./autoplay

Audio

CommandWhat it doesExample
/volume <0-100>Set the playback volume./volume 60
/effect [name]Apply an effect by name, or show the active one./effect nightcore
/effectsList every effect./effects
/resetRemove all effects. Shortcuts !fxreset !noeffect!fxreset
!bassLight bass boost.!bass
!bassboostHeavy bass boost.!bassboost
!nightcoreFaster and higher (1.25×).!nightcore
!vaporwaveSlower and lower (0.8×).!vaporwave
!trebleBoost the high frequencies.!treble
!echoAdd an echo.!echo
!8dSound that circles your head. Use headphones.!8d
!karaokeRemove the centre vocals.!karaoke

The effect shortcuts exist only with !. Under /, pick the effect from /effect. Effects can change once every three seconds per server.

Extras

CommandWhat it doesExample
/lyrics [title - artist]Follow the current song's lyrics live in one message, or look up a song by title. Shortcut !ly!ly midnight drive - neon coast
/helpShow the full command list in Discord./help
